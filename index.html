Licence questions

Can I crop or edit the photograph?

Yes, for layout and colour, without changing the work.

Can it be used to train AI?

No. Every licence excludes AI training, logos and trademarks, and removing metadata.

Is a licence exclusive?

No, unless exclusivity is agreed in writing.

Can I pass the licence to a client?

Only with Extended, to one named client.

Can I withdraw from a purchase?

Consumers have 14 days, unless they choose immediate delivery and waive it.
